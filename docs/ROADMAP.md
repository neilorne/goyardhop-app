# Roadmap to launch (March 2027)

1. **Foundation** (this version): address to lot size to instant price to book, using real county data.
2. **Hosting**: put the app online (Vercel) and point GoYardHop.com at it.
3. **Accounts and saved bookings**: customer sign-in (Apple, Google, email) and a database (Supabase).
4. **Payments**: Stripe. $10 booking fee at booking, card saved, balance charged after the customer approves the photos.
5. **Texts**: Twilio. Confirmed, en route, arrived, complete, photos ready.
6. **Crew app**: today's jobs, en route and done buttons, before and after photos.
7. **Owner dashboard**: bookings, crews, schedule, payouts, feedback.
8. **Customer extras**: photo approval with Google review invite for everyone, Hop Again, referrals.
9. **Launch prep**: terms, privacy policy, service-area ZIP codes, crew capacity, beta with neighbors.
